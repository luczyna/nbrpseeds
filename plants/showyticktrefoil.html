Desmodium canadense

Family: Fabaceae

Showy Tick Trefoil

Swink/Wilhelm #4

FLOWERING: Mid-June to mid-July

SEED TIMING: July to August

HABITAT: Moist to mesic black soil prairies, moist meadows along rivers, borders of lakes, thickets, limestone glades, and areas along railroads where prairie remnants occur

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES: About 3 feet tall, normally erect. Green central stem with fine white hairs. Compound leaves with three greyish green leaflets that are 2-3½" long, oblong to lanceolate, rounded at tips, with fine hooked hairs on undersides. Numerous pink flowers in elongated panicle at stem ends, about ½" across with upper and lower petals that open with upper petal first. Flat seedpods (loments) about 2½" long with 3-5 segments, covered with fine hooked hairs. Thickened taproot with rhizomes.

TO PICK: When seedpods have matured and the hooked hairs have detached. Pull on the white oval seed heads; ripe seeds will come off and separate into tiny seeds with parachutes.
