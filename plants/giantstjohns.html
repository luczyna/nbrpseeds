Hypericum ascyron

Family: Hypericaceae

Giant St. John's Wort

Swink/Wilhelm #N

FLOWERING: Mid-June to mid-July

SEED TIMING: July to August

HABITAT: Woodland openings, wooded slopes, banks of rivers and streams, moist thickets and meadows, river-bottom prairies, and fens

DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES: 3-5 feet tall. Erect, unbranched stems that are light green, glabrous, 4-angled, and slightly winged on the angles. Opposite leaves up to 4" long and 1½" across, lanceolate to ovate, smooth margins, glabrous, sessile or slightly clasping the stem. Upper surface medium green, lower surface pale green. Cymes of 1-5 yellow flowers at stem ends, each about 2" across with 5 spreading petals, 5 green sepals, numerous yellow stamens, and a pistil with 5 persistent styles. Large hairless seed capsules up to 1¼" long divided into 5 cells, each containing numerous small black seeds. Rhizomatous root system forming small colonies.

TO PICK: When seed capsules are fully mature and black. The capsules are divided into 5 cells, each containing numerous small black seeds. Pull the capsules from the stems.
